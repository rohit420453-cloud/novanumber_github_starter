Frequently asked questions

Helpful information about this demo marketplace.

Are these live phone numbers?

No. All listings, prices and availability shown here are sample UI data. No real number or SMS service is connected.

Does Add Funds process a real payment?

No. The payment screen is a visual preview only. It does not initiate or verify a UPI payment and does not credit a wallet.

How is my account protected?

Authentication is not implemented in this frontend-only prototype. Do not enter a real password or sensitive personal information.

How do I get help?

Open Contact Support to view the sample support dialog. A real support channel can be configured when a backend is added.
